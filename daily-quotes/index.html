Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Daily Quotes ("the App") is developed by Alan Zhang. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

Daily Quotes does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers. Your saved quotes remain on your device.

Data Storage

Your quotes are stored in a local App Group container shared with the App's widget. The same container lets Daily Quotes read quotes you previously saved in HabitTracker when both apps are installed. This data stays on your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or other third-party services.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual quotes in the App. Because the App Group is shared with HabitTracker, deleting one app may not remove the shared quotes while the other app remains installed.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
